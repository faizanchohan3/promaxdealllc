A distributor built on doing it right

PROMAXDEAL LLC is a technology and electronics distributor based in Plano, Texas. We connect manufacturers and their authorized distributors with the retailers, businesses and institutions that need their products.

Who we are

Wholesale supply, handled carefully

We are a registered Texas limited liability company in the wholesale and retail resale of technology and consumer products. From our warehouse on East Plano Parkway we receive, store and ship computer accessories, networking gear, audio, mobile power, communications equipment and test tools to customers across the United States.

Our business is built around one idea: buyers need a supplier they can rely on, and brands need resellers who protect their products. That's why we source only through manufacturers and their authorized distributors, follow every brand's pricing policies, and ship product new in its original packaging.

Whether you are stocking store shelves, outfitting an office or sourcing parts for a project, we quote quickly, confirm exact part numbers and keep you informed from order to delivery.

Our standards

How we do business

Manufacturers and distributors trust partners who protect their brands. These are the rules we follow on every product line.

  • Authorized sourcingWe buy from manufacturers and their authorized distributors. We do not sell grey-market or counterfeit goods.
  • Pricing policy complianceWe follow each brand's MAP and pricing policies, as well as any channel or territory restrictions.
  • Registered and tax compliantA registered Texas LLC holding a sales and use tax permit. Resale certificates are provided to suppliers.
  • New, original productProducts ship new, in original manufacturer packaging, with manufacturer warranty where it applies.
  • Traceable inventoryWe keep purchase records for the products we sell, so any item can be traced back to its authorized source.
  • Careful storage & handlingInventory is stored in our Plano warehouse and packed to protect product and packaging in transit.
