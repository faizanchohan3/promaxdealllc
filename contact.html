FAQ

        
Common questions

      

      

        
Do you have a minimum order?
Minimums depend on the product line and the manufacturer. Tell us what you need and we will confirm any minimum on your quote.

        
How fast will I get a quote?
Most quotes are sent within one business day. Large lists or special-order parts may take longer, and we will tell you when to expect it.

        
Do you accept purchase orders?
Yes. Purchase orders are accepted once payment terms are approved for your account.

        
Are your products genuine and new?
Yes. We buy only from manufacturers and their authorized distributors, and products ship new in original manufacturer packaging, with manufacturer warranty where it applies.

        
Can I buy tax exempt?
Yes. Provide a valid resale or exemption certificate before your order ships. Without one, applicable sales tax is added to the invoice.

        
Where do you ship?
We ship throughout the United States from our warehouse in Plano, Texas, by parcel or freight, with tracking on every shipment.

        
What is your return policy?
Returns require prior authorization, and products must be new and in original packaging. Report shortages or damage within 5 business days of delivery. See our Terms of Sale for details.

        
I'm a manufacturer or distributor. How do we work together?
Select "Manufacturer / distributor" as your buyer type in the form above, or email us. We can provide our resale certificate, registration documents and references, and sign your reseller or MAP agreement. Learn more.
