Information we collect

PROMAXDEAL LLC collects the information you provide when you request a quote or contact us, such as your name, company, email, phone number and order details. If you become a customer, we also keep the billing, shipping and tax-exemption details needed to fulfill your orders.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your inquiry, process orders and maintain your account. This includes sending quotes, confirming orders, arranging shipment, issuing invoices and meeting our tax and record-keeping obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, such as shipping carriers and payment processors, or when the law requires it.

Third-party services on this website

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows an embedded Google Map on the contact page. When your browser loads these, Google may receive your IP address and standard browser information under its own privacy policy. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

Retention & security

We keep business records for as long as needed to serve your account and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information we hold.

Your choices

To access, correct or delete your information, contact sales@promaxdeals.com. We may need to keep certain records where the law requires it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
